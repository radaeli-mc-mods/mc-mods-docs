# Radaeli Minecraft projects

Find installation and gameplay guides, compatibility details, and technical references for the projects maintained by Radaeli.

## Project catalog

### R-Ui Lib

A reusable client-side interface library for Minecraft mods.

- **Minecraft:** 1.20.1 and 1.21.1
- **Loaders:** Forge and NeoForge
- **Guides:** installation, requirements, integration, components, themes, and examples

[Open R-Ui Lib documentation](/en/mods/r-ui-lib/)

### Aeronautics: Fly by Instruments

Avionics sensors and CC:Tweaked integrations for Create Aeronautics.

- **Minecraft:** 1.21.1
- **Loader:** NeoForge
- **Version:** 0.3.0
- **Guides:** sensors, avionics, wiring, Ponder tutorials, configuration, and Computer Craft

[Open Aeronautics: Fly by Instruments documentation](/en/mods/fly-by-instruments/)

### Hotbar Locker

Protect selected hotbar slots from automatic item replacement.

- **Minecraft:** 1.20.1 and 1.21.1
- **Loaders:** Forge for 1.20.1; Fabric, Forge, and NeoForge for 1.21.1
- **Guides:** installation, slot locks, and settings

[Open Hotbar Locker documentation](/en/mods/hotbar-locker/)

### Sophisticated Backpacks: Digital Storage Expansion

Connect backpack upgrades to AE2, Refined Storage, Tom's Simple Storage, or SCC on NeoForge 1.21.1.

- **Minecraft:** 1.20.1 and 1.21.1
- **Loaders:** Forge for 1.20.1; NeoForge for 1.21.1
- **Guides:** linking, filters, and eight upgrades

[Open Digital Storage Expansion documentation](/en/mods/sophisticated-backpack-is-addon/)

### Create: Copycat Replace!

Swap Copycat materials directly or apply, replace, and remove matching connected materials.

- **Minecraft:** 1.20.1 and 1.21.1
- **Loaders:** Forge for 1.20.1; NeoForge for 1.21.1
- **Version:** 0.3.0
- **Guides:** installation, selection modes, inventory materials, and configuration

[Open Create: Copycat Replace! documentation](/en/mods/copycat-replace/)
