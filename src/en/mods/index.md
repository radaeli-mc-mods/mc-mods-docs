# Project catalog

This catalog grows as each project's documentation is reviewed and added.

## R-Ui Lib

A reusable client-side interface library for Minecraft mods.

- **Minecraft:** 1.20.1 and 1.21.1
- **Loaders:** Forge and NeoForge
- **For players:** installation, requirements, and library overview
- **For developers:** integration, component reference, themes, and development validation

[Open R-Ui Lib documentation](/en/mods/r-ui-lib/index.md)

## Aeronautics: Fly by Instruments

Avionics sensors, attitude instruments and CC:Tweaked interfaces for Create Aeronautics.

- **Minecraft:** 1.21.1
- **Loader:** NeoForge
- **Version:** 0.4.0-beta.2
- **For players:** sensors, attitude instruments, the Avionics Computer and GPWS, Modular Table, Avionics Wire, Ponder tutorials, configuration, and Computer Craft

[Open Aeronautics: Fly by Instruments documentation](/en/mods/fly-by-instruments/index.md)

## Hotbar Locker

Protect selected hotbar slots from automatic item replacement.

- **Minecraft:** 1.20.1 and 1.21.1
- **Loaders:** Forge for 1.20.1; Fabric, Forge, and NeoForge for 1.21.1
- **For players:** installation, slot locks, and settings

[Open Hotbar Locker documentation](/en/mods/hotbar-locker/index.md)

## Create: On Demand

Request products through the Stock Keeper using recipes and ingredient connections already set up on Factory Gauges.

- **Minecraft:** 1.20.1 and 1.21.1
- **Loaders:** Forge for 1.20.1; NeoForge for 1.21.1
- **Version:** 0.1.1
- **For players:** installation, ordering, delivery, and configuration

[Open Create: On Demand documentation](/en/mods/create-on-demand/index.md)

## Sophisticated Backpacks: Digital Storage Expansion

Connect Sophisticated Backpacks upgrades to AE2, Refined Storage, Tom's Simple Storage, or SCC on NeoForge 1.21.1.

- **Minecraft:** 1.20.1 and 1.21.1
- **Loaders:** Forge for 1.20.1; NeoForge for 1.21.1
- **For players:** linking, filters, and a catalog of nine upgrades

[Open Digital Storage Expansion documentation](/en/mods/sophisticated-backpack-is-addon/index.md)

## Create: Copycat Replace!

Swap Copycat materials directly or apply, replace, and remove matching connected materials.

- **Minecraft:** 1.20.1 and 1.21.1
- **Loaders:** Forge and Fabric for 1.20.1, NeoForge for 1.21.1
- **Version:** 0.3.1
- **Guides:** installation, selection modes, inventory materials, and configuration

[Open Create: Copycat Replace! documentation](/en/mods/copycat-replace/)

## RoadWeaver

Roads between structures with Tectonic 3.0.28 terrain.

- **Minecraft:** 1.21.1
- **Loader:** NeoForge
- **Version:** 2.4.1-1.21.1

[Open fork documentation](/en/mods/roadweaver-tectonic3/)
