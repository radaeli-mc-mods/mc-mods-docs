# Create: On Demand

[![CurseForge](https://cf.way2muchnoise.eu/title/1758836.svg)](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/create-on-demand)

Create: On Demand lets you request products from the **Stock Keeper** using recipes and ingredient connections already configured on your **Factory Gauges**. The factory crafts the requested quantity and its connected components, reusing ingredients in storage. No new blocks, items or recipes are added.

**Documentation reflects Create: On Demand 0.1.1.**

| Minecraft | Loader | Create |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1.20.1 | Forge 47.4.0 | 6.0.8 |
| 1.21.1 | NeoForge 21.1.236 | 6.0.10 |

Install the matching mod version on both the client and server.
