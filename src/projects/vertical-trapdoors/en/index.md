# Vertical Trapdoors

[![CurseForge](https://cf.way2muchnoise.eu/title/1787624.svg)](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/vertical-trapdoors)

Place trapdoors upright against walls and choose which way they open. You can also use trapdoors added by other mods.
